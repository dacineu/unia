## A note on what the messages contain

Recorded because it is easy to read the transcript as a sequence of instructions and
miss what it actually is: the four substantive messages are not a specification, they
are four **corrections to a design I had already built and mis-measured**.

- Message 1 said the economy was inflationary, and was right. Twelve identical feeds
  had taken the power from 0.500 to 0.694.
- Message 2 arrived after I had been building a game for two days without opening
  `src/fluid/`, `src/registry/` or `src/meta_actuators/` — the market that already
  answered most of what I thought was an open blocker.
- Message 3 asked whether the fixture was a query set, and thereby dissolved the
  problem I had twice refused to solve, rather than answering it.
- Message 4 said the scope gradient is bidirectional and reversible when credit
  holds — which was the rule that turned `allowed_scopes` from `[preferred, Global]`
  into a function of demonstrated credit.

Three of those four were things I had asserted were missing. That pattern is the
reason the session log gives the wrong assertions as much space as the right ones.
